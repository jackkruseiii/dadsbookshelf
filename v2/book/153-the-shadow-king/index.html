From the shelf

Following Mussolini's invasion to avenge Italy's 19th-century embarrassment at the Battle of Adua, Kidane assembles a local militia at exiled Emperor Haile Selassie's behest. His wife Aster leads the women who trail the fighters supplying food and bullets, and eventually convinces him to let her women fight. Mengiste brings to light a forgotten piece of Ethiopia's resistance against Italy — the women who fought and were written out of the history. Her talent has continued to grow since her debut, evident in stunning lines throughout.

“They have come to rewrite history, to alter memory, to resurrect their dead and refashion them as heroes.”

“She is one woman. She is many women. She is all the sound that exists in the world.”

“We were the Shadow King. We were those who stepped into a country left dark by an invading plague and gave new hope to Ethiopia's people.”
