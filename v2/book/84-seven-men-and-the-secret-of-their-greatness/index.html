Metaxas hits his literary stride in 7 Men, offering a very readable and concise portrait of seven key figures in the Christian faith and the global stage. 7 Men contains echoes of all these elements as the reader meets Jackie Robinson, Chuck Colson, William Wilberforce, Bonhoeffer, George Washington, Eric Liddell and Pope John Paul II.

“Who can imagine that the liberty of millions might depend on the character of one man?”
