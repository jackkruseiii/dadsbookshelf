You will learn about the incredible journeys that the original European adventurers made as they explored the previously "undiscovered" interior of Africa. In Praise of Savagery is the memoir of a young man who befriended the great British explorer Wilfred Thesiger. Eventually, Cairns gets the opportunity to essentially retrace Thesiger's original steps when he first set out to find the origin of the Awash River in Ethiopia.

“The point is this. Wilfred Thesiger was a man who made a difference to the world.”
