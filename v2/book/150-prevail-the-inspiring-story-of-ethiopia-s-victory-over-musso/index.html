From the yearly reading list

I can't imagine there's a more well-researched book out there on Ethiopia's decades-long resistance and eventual defeat of an overeager, over confident, overreaching wanna-be colonial power. Canadian author Jeff Pearce's work is strongest as he brings to life the myriad actors, antagonists, and heroes of the saga. It's worth noting that Pearce is a bit of a Selassie apologist, but given the complicated history of Ras Tafari Makkonen's reign, I imagine this can fall either way.
