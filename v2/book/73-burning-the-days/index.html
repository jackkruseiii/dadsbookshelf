Ultimately Burning the Days is not a memoir about anyone but Salter and his own quest (or pursuit) of immortality … his outsized power in description--there's no author who does in better today. My favorite part … are his descriptions of favorite books and authors--I added about 10 new books to my Amazon wish list!

“Art, in a sense, is life brought to a standstill, rescued from time.”
