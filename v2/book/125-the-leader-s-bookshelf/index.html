From the yearly reading list

Jim Stavridis' gift to bookavores everywhere, The Leader's Bookshelf is difficult to take notes on because it's so packed with great and essential material. Stavridis and former Navy PAO R. Ancell put together a Top 50 reading list for military leaders by surveying hundreds of senior military leaders, then ranked the most frequently cited titles by frequency of citation. As a FAO, I enjoyed Stavridis' analyses that repeatedly noted the need for regional expertise to better inform national strategic decision-making.

“Outside solutions unanchored by an understanding of a given regional system are almost always doomed to fail.”
