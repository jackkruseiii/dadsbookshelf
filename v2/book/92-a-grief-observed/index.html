From the yearly reading list

Lewis recounts an honest and open account of one man's reckoning with the loss of his wife--tragically he only had a few short years with her before she died after a battle with cancer. This is one of those books that you hope you never have to be glad you read. What makes this short book special is Lewis' honesty, as his wife's death causes him to question everything that he believes, including his own faith. Ultimately, Lewis emerges from his sorrow with a deepened faith.
