An eye-opening, and heart-opening, look at one woman's experiences growing up and working in "a world made for whiteness." She writes beautifully and with a vulnerability and urgency that asks the reader to wake up and cast aside complacency.

“I need a love that is troubled by injustice...a love that has no tolerance for hate, no excuses for racist decisions, no contentment in the status quo.”
