From the yearly reading list

Yes, this was on all the best-seller lists, but most importantly this is the first book that Emily and I ever read together. Beautiful imagery and relatable metaphors peripherally address the issue of why bad things happen. I say peripherally because in reading the book you realize that you will never get an answer to this question because it's the wrong question to ask in the first place.
