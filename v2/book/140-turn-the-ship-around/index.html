From the yearly reading list

I let this book rest dormant on my Amazon wishlist for months because of my unfair bias against submariners, but I wasn't even a chapter in when I realized how misguided that bias had been. Midway through, retired Captain David Marquet shares the story of a nighttime passage through the strait of Malacca that nearly resulted in a collision, averted by the timely actions of one of his petty officers--whom Marquet immediately awarded a Navy Achievement Medal on the spot. This floored me, since in my own experience recognition like that almost always arrives a year late, if at all.

“Don't move information to authority, move authority to information.”
