An incredible tale of friendship, faith and sacrifice. Charles Spurgeon is, of course, the renowned "prince of preachers." Authors Matt Carter and Aaron Ivey tell the story of his friendship with African-American missionary Thomas Johnson against the backdrop of Spurgeon's own struggles with depression, anxiety and gout.
