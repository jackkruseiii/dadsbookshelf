One of my favorite things is to have a writer I love tell me about writers he loves. Having just discovered writers' writer Salter a year ago, I was happy to go down the rabbit hole and find out who this writer was that Salter thought worth mentioning in his own biography. I was impressed by Kennaway's story of one man's gradual descent into destruction, but what struck me most about Living Like This was the author's descriptive abilities.

“Before we are infected, by love or measles, we are wide open to infection.”
