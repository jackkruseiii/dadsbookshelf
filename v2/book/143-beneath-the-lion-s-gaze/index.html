From the yearly reading list

One of the only African nations never to have been colonized, one of Ethiopia's darkest periods came during the 13-year socialist rule of the Derg. This military junta overthrew an increasingly oblivious Haile Selassie and set about consolidating power under the leadership of despot Mengistu, and Beneath the Lion's Gaze retells this period through the eyes of one Ethiopian family. The family's patriarch is a renowned doctor ordered to keep a barely-alive torture victim alive so she can be tortured further; his son becomes a freedom fighter known as "Mekonnen Killer of Soldiers." Author Maaza Mengiste uses this one family's struggles to bring to life the experience of Ethiopians who starved under Selassie, only to be persecuted and killed under the Derg and starved again under Mengistu's reign.
