From the yearly reading list

I first read this book back in 2015 and it's so important to fathers that I put it on my yearly re-reading list, although it took me four years to actually read it again. In my second reading it proved just as powerful, profound and insightful. The bottom line is that you can't underestimate the role of a father in his daughters' lives--you have to fight for her, engage with her, and continually draw her close throughout her life, particularly during those teen years. Don't worry about what other parents do; worry about your daughter.

“Dad, it’s not optional: your daughter needs you to be her hero.”
