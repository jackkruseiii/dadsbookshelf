In this memoir, French author Emmanuel Carrere takes issue with Fitzgerald's well known assertion that "all life is a process of breaking down" as he peers into the life of Juliette, carefully building a picture of her circling journey from tragedy to love to passion and back to tragedy as her cancer returns to deal its final blow.

“That is the worst of fates: never to have been seen, never to have been acknowledged.”
