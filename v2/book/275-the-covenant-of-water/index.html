Spanning nearly a century (1900-1977), Covenant takes place in southwestern India in the Kerala region and focuses on a Malayali Christian family and a mysterious drowning affliction that affects someone in each generation.

“This is the covenant of water: that they're all linked inescapably by their acts of commission and omission, and no one stands alone... all is one.”
