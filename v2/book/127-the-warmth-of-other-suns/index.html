From the shelf

Read it Because: You can't afford not to. Before the publication of Warmth some seven years ago, there had never been a definitive and comprehensive analysis of these waves of migration that began following the reconstruction era as Jim Crow set in. Let the following statement sink in: Just 77 years ago, in 1/3 of the US, killing blacks was not a crime. Now while I may not agree with every single tenet of the #blacklivesmatter movement, reading this book opened my eyes to the historical headspace from which its leaders and followers are coming. Much of the conflict in today's society would at least be softened if both sides took the time to know one another's history. Wilkerson has presented a gift to Americans.
