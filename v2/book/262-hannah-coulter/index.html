Hannah Coulter, the penultimate novel in Wendell Berry's Port Williams series, offers a touching first-person account of its title character's life … It's a deep narrative, full of sorrow and triumph, heartache and love, pain and healing … reading it as a husband and father at the midpoint of his life feels like a clarion call.
