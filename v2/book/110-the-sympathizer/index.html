From the yearly reading list

You get to view the Vietnam War from a totally different perspective--in this case, that of a South Vietnamese officer who is actually an American-educated spy for the communist North. Other themes addressed against the backdrop of the war include poverty, Hollywood "yellow-washing," torture, love-making and billiards, confession, forgetting and happiness as a zero-sum game in war.
