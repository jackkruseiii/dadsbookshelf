From the yearly reading list

"We all were happy here once," reminisces Kai, the local Freetown surgeon, after the death of his last link to the time before. With beautiful, striking prose, author Aminatta Forna reveals the soul of a nation where nearly everyone is stricken with what the West calls PTSD, but as one local notes, "You call it a disorder, my friend. We call it life." Forna does not give in to easy storylines about the courage of the war's survivors--it is the memory of Sierra Leone's before that gives people the strength to slowly put their lives together again.
