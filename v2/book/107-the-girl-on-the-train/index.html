From the yearly reading list

You can finish it in three days (or in one day at the beach), it's that fast of a read. Aside from being a pretty dark novel this is a perfect beach read. This story touches upon all the major nerves: falling in love, betrayal, jealousy, depression, murder, delusion--you get it all. Hawkins does a great job masking the outcome from the reader--this is the pull that keeps the pages turning.

“Nobody warned me it would break us. But it did.”
