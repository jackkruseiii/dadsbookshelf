From the yearly reading list

Read it because you get to peer into the birth of feminism for a Muslim woman in post-independence Senegal. It took me a little bit to get into this story, mainly because I'd never read a book whose format was a simple, lengthy meandering letter. Ultimately, Ba won me over with her tender, thoughtful introspective look at the life of a cuckolded, then sister-wifed, then widowed, then independent and finally (maybe) free woman.

“Friendship has splendours that love knows not.”
