I've enjoyed reading Matt Haig's novels over the last several years, and when I saw he had written a very raw memoir I was eager to read it. That it turned out to also be a "how to be a friend to someone suffering from depression" guide made it an especially powerful and necessary read.

“Misery, like yoga, is not a competitive sport.”
