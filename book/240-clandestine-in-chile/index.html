Don't recommend. I really looked forward to this novel since I've always loved the magical realism of Gabriel Garcia Marquez, and while there's some beautiful writing, this journalistic retelling of a filmmaker's secret mission to film inside Chile and expose the brutality of life under Pinochet reads less like a spy novel and more like an ambling fait accompli.
