From the yearly reading list

I read author Andy Weir's blockbuster debut novel The Martian back in 2015 and loved it, so when Artemis came out I added it to my Audible queue. Artemis is a colony on the moon, and the story focuses on a young smuggler named Jazz, raised on the moon, who develops a pirat-ish code of conduct aimed at moving up the stratified social order. Jazz irked me at times--she comes across as a little too enamored with just how cute and clever she is--but Weir has done what talented authors do: create a previously unknown world and open its door to readers everywhere.
