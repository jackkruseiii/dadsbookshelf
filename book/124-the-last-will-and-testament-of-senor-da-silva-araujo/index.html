From the yearly reading list

This translated piece of Cape Verdean fiction is beautiful, sad, and meandering as it examines the life of a poor peasant boy made good as a successful business owner. Senor da Silva's life unfolds in a non-linear, often-dreamlike fashion as we follow him through the reading of his last will and testament. Far more interesting, though, is the investigation by da Silva's illegitimate daughter into a mysterious love obsession named Adelia. As the details are slowly revealed, a melancholy shadow falls over da Silva as he grapples with love, rejection and loss.
