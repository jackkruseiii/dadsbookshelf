Let me just get it out of the way and say that Chapter 15 will break your heart. The Twelve Lives of Samuel Hawley is a modern day adaptation of the ancient Greek tale of the 12 Labors of Hercules, and the genius of author Hannah Tinti's story is that she layers several tragic love stories onto this epic framework. Mechanically, the novel overlays the history of the 12 bullets that struck Samuel Hawley during his life with his daughter Lou's own coming of age, as she slowly uncovers her father's sordid criminal past.

“Everything breaks if you hit it hard enough.”
