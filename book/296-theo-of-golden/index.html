From the shelf

There's not much allegorical fiction being written these days. Theo of Golden makes me want a lot more of it — at least more of it written by Allen Levi. Published in 2023 but somehow I only heard of the novel a month ago from a pastor friend named Steve. The story is about an octogenarian named Theo who mysteriously arrives in the town of Golden and proceeds to — incredibly — get to know perfect strangers! He uses the gift of hand drawn portraits as the mechanism in this quest and along the way offers a winsome example of kindness in action.

“God gave us faces so we can see each other better.”
