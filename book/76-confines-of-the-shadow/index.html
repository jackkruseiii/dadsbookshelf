From the yearly reading list

Read it because you want to understand European colonialism in a deeper context, or because you love beautiful, witty writing in historical fiction. Alessandro Spina was a Syrian Maronite who grew up in Benghazi, was educated and wrote in Italian, and over 40 years penned an extraordinary cycle of novels about the establishment, flourishing and troubled aftermath of the Italian colony in Libya.

This book is also catalogued on Beyond Achebe, a literary atlas of Africa.
