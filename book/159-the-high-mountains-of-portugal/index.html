Man Booker prize winning author Yann Martel has crafted a trio of related novellas that lay bare the trauma of loss and its psychic weight that can echo across generations like a deep curse. We join the journey of three characters: a bereaved widower on a mission to find a lost African crucifix; a grieving, delusional pathologist obsessed with Agatha Christie; and an aging widower/politician who buys a chimpanzee and retreats with the animal to his family's ancestral village.

“And now, how do I dare love anything?”
