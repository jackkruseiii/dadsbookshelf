From the shelf

Before Adichie was regaling the world with her story of a relatively privileged immigrant experience in 'Americanah', Ethiopian author Dinaw Mengestu was pulling back the curtain on a much bleaker immigrant experience. The title comes from the closing lines of Dante's Inferno. In the case of the narrator Sepha and his friends, they have escaped the violence in their own country and arrived in America, 'seeing the stars.' Only they soon discover that while their new life in the United States may not be hell, it will likely be much more like the penance of purgatory than that of paradiso.

“As it was, I did not come to America to find a better life. I came here running and screaming with the ghosts of an old one firmly attached to my back.”

“All the best dictators are colonels.”
