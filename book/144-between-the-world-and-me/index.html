From the yearly reading list

This is a love letter from a father to a son, written with the urgency of a man who knows he may perish at any moment. Coates writes beautifully as he recalls his youth and experiences as a father--his memoir reflects serious inner self-examination as he seeks to create a blueprint for his son to survive, and possibly even thrive, in a world Coates argues is stacked against him from a historical, institutional, and psycho-cultural perspective. As Coates warns his son to keep his guard up, he adds: "Never forget that we were enslaved in this country longer than we have been free."
