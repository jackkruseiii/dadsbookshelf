From the yearly reading list

Irony that Achebe writes a novel about the disconnect between the elite and the poor masses at such a level that it is not accessible by those same masses because of the higher intellectual level at which it is written. … Older Abazonian man's speech is a throwback to Achebe's prior novels (and a breath of fresh air).
