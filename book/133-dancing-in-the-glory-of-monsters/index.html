From the yearly reading list

I listened to this lengthy journalistic endeavor by writer Jason Stearns as he attempts to unravel the most complicated conflict the world never cared about: the two wars in the Congo. As Stearns digs deeper you are quickly struck by the overwhelming intensity of violence--the sheer level of sexual violence is incomprehensible, and there's likely no one in the country of 64 million who doesn't know someone who was raped or assaulted. Ultimately, it is the absence of any justice or effective state institutions that marks the Congo's conflicts as distinct from those elsewhere in Africa.

“Who has not been Mobutists in this country? We saw you all, dancing in the glory of the monster.”
