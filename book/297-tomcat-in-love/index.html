Tim O'Brien can write. His sentence and dialogue and paragraph flow masterfully--he's a quick read, there's no Faulkner-esque chewing of paragraphs required to digest. But after every book I read, I consider the fundamental reader's question: would I recommend it? After I finished Tomcat and jotted down some key quotes that captured some superb writing, I tossed it in the trash.

“Nothing is as important as a likable narrator. Nothing holds a story together better.”
