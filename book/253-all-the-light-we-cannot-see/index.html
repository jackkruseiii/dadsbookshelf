From the yearly reading list

The main character, a blind teenager named Marie-Laure, questions as the Nazi Paris invasion uproots her comfortable and predictable life. What unfolds is a story that seesaws between shocking and inspiring, horrifying and redemptive and which stands as a stark reminder of what a world at war looks like at the most visceral level. As I tore through this novel in 3 quick days, I lamented that I'd let it waste away for so long on our bookshelf.

“Who knew love could kill you?”
