Saramago is a Nobel Prize winner, so if I wasn't crazy about his book something must be wrong with me, I guess. Ultimately, All the Names is a story about identity and the significance of remembering, following a beaten-down aging clerk at a Department of Births and Deaths in a Portuguese town who discovers the card of a woman and sets out to find out who she is. For me, though, this novel lacked action--the book turns out to be a character study of the clerk. So if you love a good character study, this is your book.
