From the shelf

A phenomenal and important read. I listened to it on Audible and about 1/3 of the way through I found it so incredible that I ordered a physical copy so I could reread it and make extensive notes. Author David Brooks offers a framework by which one could be not a better leader but a more caring and empathetic friend and person in general. I particularly enjoyed some of the questions he uses to better understand where people come from. One example: In your family what was one thing you could never do?
