Tim Keller stepped down as senior pastor at the renowned Redeemer Church in NYC in early 2017, the church he built into 5000 strong members in one of the most unchurched areas of the United States. Hidden Christmas is the product of a lifetime of Keller's Christmas sermons, covering the breadth of the Christmas story from its roots in the Old Testament. I finished it with a newfound understanding for just how significant it was that God became man--that all that had to happen for the people of Israel to approach God was to merely come, and adore him, in a manger, as a defenseless baby.

“The manger at Christmas means that, if you live like Jesus, there won't be room for you in a lot of inns.”
