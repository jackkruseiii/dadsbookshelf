From the yearly reading list

It gives you the inside scoop on the culture and politics of Madagascar. Soamiely is the rare Malagasy author who writes in (great) English about these things. Vakio Milamina lays out the author's thoughts on life in Madagascar through a series of very loosely related essays. In particular I loved "It's All Part of Growing Up," basically a 6 page rundown on the political history of Madagascar, and "Our Unambiguously Ambiguous Art of Conversation," in which he lays out why a Malagasy will seldom ever say 'no.'
